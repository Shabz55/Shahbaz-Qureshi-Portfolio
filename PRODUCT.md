# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React with Vite and TypeScript.

## Users

Employers evaluating Shahbaz Qureshi's work and background.

## Product Purpose

This is Shahbaz Qureshi's personal portfolio. It gives employers a clear way to explore his projects and understand his experience. Success means employers can quickly find and meaningfully evaluate the projects.

## Positioning

The portfolio is organized around direct exploration of Shahbaz's actual projects and supporting resume content rather than invented claims or generic portfolio filler.

## Operating Context

Visitors review the portfolio on the web while evaluating a potential candidate. Project exploration is the primary interaction and decision path.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the existing projects.
- Preserve the existing resume content.
- The current implementation is a React/Vite TypeScript web app.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing project content in `src/data.ts` and `src/App.tsx`.
- Project media in `assets/media/`.
- Resume PDF at `public/Shahbaz_Qureshi_Resume.pdf`.

## Product Principles

- Make the projects easy for employers to discover and evaluate.
- Let real work and evidence lead the experience.
- Preserve the accuracy of the existing projects and resume.
- Keep the experience clear and usable across the web.
